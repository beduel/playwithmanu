The campaign

Become a Pokémon Trainer and explore a region full of discovery.

Routes connect towns, forests, caves and remote areas with their own Pokémon populations, characters and local stories. The world is open to exploration and rewards curiosity.

Catching Pokémon is one part of the journey. Training, team composition, exploration and relationships with your Pokémon develop throughout the campaign. Battles reward preparation and understanding what each member of your team can do.

Gyms, competitions, researchers, criminal organizations and rival trainers create longer storylines. Rumours can lead to rare species, hidden places and unusual events across the region, keeping discovery and progression at the heart of the adventure.
