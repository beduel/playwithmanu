The campaign

Become a Hunter and enter a world where skill and information open doors.

The Hunter Exam is only the beginning. Licensed Hunters gain access to restricted places, valuable information and opportunities that can lead into unexplored territory, criminal networks, rare creature hunts and conflicts involving powerful Nen users.

Nen develops as a personal expression of each character. Abilities grow from individual strengths, limitations and ideas, giving fights a strong tactical element. Understanding an opponent can be more important than raw power.

Investigations, competitions, expeditions and dangerous assignments become major arcs connected by recurring characters and organizations. The setting and concepts of Hunter × Hunter provide the foundation for an original group of Hunters and their own story.
