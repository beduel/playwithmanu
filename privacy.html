Privacy

playwithmanu.com is a promotional website for online tabletop RPG campaigns.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is used only after you choose “Accept analytics.” It helps measure visits, page usage, traffic sources, and outbound clicks such as links to StartPlaying, including the campaign page and link text used, so I can understand which campaign pages generate booking interest. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics script is not loaded by this site.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it. You can reopen the choice below at any time.

External services

Links to StartPlaying and other external websites are governed by those services’ own privacy practices once you leave this site.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options available through the linked StartPlaying profile.
