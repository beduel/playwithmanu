FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I book a game?

Choose a campaign and book your seat through StartPlaying.

When do I pay?

You only pay when you play. Payments are handled through StartPlaying.

Are beginners welcome?

Absolutely. No previous D&D or TTRPG experience is required.

What do I need to play?

A computer, a stable internet connection, a microphone and a free Roll20 account.

How long is a session?

Sessions last 3 hours.

Which language do we play in?

Games are played primarily in English.

Do I need to know the setting?

No. Everything you need to know will be introduced during the game.

Can I join an ongoing campaign?

Yes. You can join a campaign at any time, and I’ll help introduce your character naturally into the story.
